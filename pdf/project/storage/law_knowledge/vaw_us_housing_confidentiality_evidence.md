# United States Federal Reference: Housing, Confidentiality, and Evidence

Jurisdiction: United States federal. Verify state, local, tribal, territorial, and program-specific rules.

Housing protections:

- VAWA-related federal housing provisions protect victims of domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault, and stalking in covered housing programs.
- Covered housing rules can address admission, eviction, termination, emergency transfer, confidentiality, and documentation.
- Documentation may include a signed self-certification, a signed statement from a victim service provider, attorney, medical professional, or mental health professional, law enforcement or court records, or other evidence accepted by the housing provider or agency.

Confidentiality and privacy:

- Personally identifying information can include information likely to disclose a victim's location or identity.
- Confidentiality is central in domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault, and stalking matters because disclosure may increase danger.
- RAG outputs should avoid exposing shelter location, exact safe address, phone number, or identifying information unless the user explicitly needs it and it is safe.

Evidence triage:

- Stronger evidence may include protection orders, police reports, medical or forensic records, photographs with timestamps, text messages, voicemails, emails, social media messages, location data, witness statements, housing notices, school or workplace records, and prior incident logs.
- Digital evidence should preserve metadata when possible. Screenshots are useful but can be weaker than exports or records with source metadata.
- Medical and mental health evidence may be sensitive. Summaries should be minimal and privacy-preserving.

RAG safety behavior:

- If retrieved documents mention immediate danger, strangulation, weapon threats, stalking escalation, kidnapping/confinement, child danger, suicidal or homicidal threats, or violation of a protection order, prioritize safety planning and urgent professional help.
- Do not advise confronting the alleged abuser.
- Flag missing jurisdiction, dates, relationship status, location, age, existing orders, and whether police/medical/court records exist.
