# Violence Against Women Reference Pack

Scope: domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault, stalking, technology-facilitated abuse, evidence issues, survivor protection, and legal-service triage.

Use: retrieval context for the local legal RAG pipeline. This pack is jurisdiction-tagged. The system should not treat international, United States federal, and Tunisia law as interchangeable.

Emergency rule: if a person is in immediate danger, contact emergency services first. Legal analysis should not delay safety planning, medical care, police reporting when chosen by the survivor, or access to victim services.

Jurisdiction tags:

- United States federal: VAWA definitions, OVW focus areas, housing confidentiality and documentation protections.
- International: UN Declaration on the Elimination of Violence against Women, CEDAW General Recommendation No. 35, WHO and CDC context.
- Tunisia: Organic Law No. 58 of 2017 on eliminating violence against women.

Core issues to retrieve:

- Relationship context: current or former spouse, intimate partner, dating partner, co-parent, cohabitant, family/household relation, or other covered relationship.
- Conduct type: physical, sexual, psychological, emotional, economic, technological, stalking, coercive control, threats, deprivation of liberty, harassment, exploitation, or retaliation.
- Evidence type: medical record, police report, court order, protection order, signed professional statement, survivor statement, text messages, screenshots, photographs, injury documentation, witness statements, housing or employment records.
- Risk flags: strangulation/suffocation, escalating threats, stalking, weapons, pregnancy, isolation, economic control, technology monitoring, threats to children/pets, suicide/homicide threats, forced sex, confinement, or violation of a protection order.
- Legal action categories: immediate safety, protection orders, criminal complaint, family court, housing protections, confidentiality, immigration or victim services referral, evidence preservation, medical forensic exam, and legal aid.
