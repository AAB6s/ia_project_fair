Document evidence and legal context pipeline

A complete evidence-processing system: pretrained computer vision models extract document context, a local retrieval layer grounds the legal references, and a local language model produces a structured Tunisia-focused legal triage report.

03 / Training Notebook

How the final notebook is organized

Data controls

Label mapping, class caps, deduplication, train/validation/test split, and task-safe augmentation reduce leakage and imbalance.

Transfer learning

Pretrained backbones are trained in a frozen phase, then fully fine-tuned with smaller backbone learning rates.

Reliability layer

EMA comparison, temperature scaling, validation-only decision tuning, and calibration metrics are included before export.

TaskFinal splitNotes
CNN1 content74,198 after dedup, 51,938 train / 11,130 validation / 11,130 testMain 10-class model for document/evidence type.
CNN2 evidence41,482 after dedup, 29,036 train / 6,223 validation / 6,223 testEvidence role model trained with proxy labels and CNN1 embedding support.
CNN3 quality22,125 total, 15,487 train / 3,319 validation / 3,319 testQuality labels are ordinal and naturally subjective.
CNN4 tamper29,776 after dedup, 20,842 train / 4,467 validation / 4,467 testBalanced authentic/tampered screening task.

Training strategy

Frozen phase

Lock pretrained features and train the new classification head first.

Full fine-tune

Unfreeze the backbone with a smaller learning rate than the head.

Regularize

Use AdamW, weight decay, dropout, class weighting, label smoothing, safe MixUp/CutMix, and early stopping.

Calibrate

Fit temperature scaling on validation logits to improve confidence reliability.

Tune decisions

Use validation-only threshold or logit-bias tuning, then keep the method only if it improves validation F1.

Export

Save checkpoints, model card, comparison files, learning curves, confusion matrices, calibration plots, local explanations, and integration schema.

06 / RAG And Legal Feedback

How legal grounding works

Local legal library

Legal references live in project/storage/law_knowledge/. The current demo focuses on violence-against-women legal triage with Tunisia references, especially Organic Law No. 58 of 2017.

Retrieval index

The app chunks legal references, builds a local retrieval index, expands the query toward Tunisian law, and returns cited source chunks for the answer.

Document context

The retrieved legal chunks are combined with extracted facts, OCR text, content route, evidence role, readability label, and authenticity label.

Guarded output

The final report avoids unsupported article numbers, unsupported codes, and legal overclaims. If the local model is unavailable, a deterministic cited fallback report is returned.

08 / Reliability Boundaries

What the system can and cannot claim

Reliable for

Structured document context, evidence type separation, scan-quality labeling, authenticity screening, visual explainability artifacts, and local retrieval input.

Not a replacement for

Legal counsel, forensic lab verification, court admissibility decisions, jurisdiction-specific deadlines, or proof that an allegation is true.

Interpretation rule: model scores describe held-out visual classification performance. Legal decisions still require current law, verified facts, and qualified human review.
